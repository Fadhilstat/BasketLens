What was excluded, and why?

30,317 routine line exclusions and another 5,773 eligible sale lines across conflicting invoices were excluded from the analytical cohort. Full definitions and precedence are documented in the methodology and reproducible code.

03 / Commercial interpretation

Turn evidence into a responsible test

Recommendation, not forecast

Analyst recommendation

Test the pairing.
Do not assume return.

Co-purchase recurrence is a reason to assess a candidate pairing under controlled conditions, not to change placement immediately.

A / Feasibility

Check stock and margin.

Confirm compatible inventory, expected margin, return rates and placement costs.

B / Experiment

Compare against control.

Randomise eligible exposure to optional adjacent placement or bundling.

C / Outcome

Measure actual lift.

Track completed purchases, contribution margin and customer guardrails.

Evidence boundary

This project describes historical co-occurrence, not incremental revenue, causal conversion uplift, profitability or current consumer preferences. No A/B experiment was performed.
