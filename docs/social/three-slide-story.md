# BasketLens | Three-slide consulting story

This publication asset contains exactly three landscape PNG slides exported at 1920 x 1080 pixels. The numbered PNGs and editable PowerPoint are delivered separately as the M6.2 publication package.

## Slide 1: From historical receipts to a retail decision worth testing

Figures: 1,067,371 original source lines, 40,280 eligible invoice baskets, and 1,500 training-selected rules in the public exhibit.

Question: Which pairs are sufficiently consistent to justify a controlled retail test?

Alt text: Editorial forest-green slide showing the analysis path from historical receipts to cleaned baskets and curated training-selected association rules.

## Slide 2: One pairing repeats after the training cutoff

Jumbo Bag Pink Polkadot (22386) to Jumbo Bag Red Retrospot (85099B).

Earlier training: 1,166 joint baskets, confidence 63.1%, lift 6.24x.

Later holdout: 279 of 416 bags, confidence 67.1%, lift 6.77x.

The pair is chosen with training data before looking at the later period.

Alt text: Green confidence bars comparing 63.1% earlier against 67.1% later, beside a dark-green later-period card showing 279 of 416 co-occurring baskets.

## Slide 3: A test, not a revenue forecast

Validate unit economics and availability. Randomise placement or bundling against a control. Measure outcomes and guardrails, with sample size and metrics defined in advance.

No causal revenue, conversion or profit uplift has been measured. Data are historical, from 2009 to 2011.

Alt text: A three-stage consulting recommendation and a prominent warning that no incremental commercial impact was measured.
