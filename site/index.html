02 / HOW THE EVIDENCE WAS BUILT

From receipts to an auditable shortlist.

Keep selection and evaluation separate. Exclude ineligible lines, quarantine inconsistent invoices and preserve each valid basket in its chronological period.

Build valid baskets

40,280 eligible invoice baskets. 83 conflicting invoices quarantined.

03

Discover, then test

32,224 earlier baskets for model selection; 8,056 later baskets for descriptive holdout checks.

04

Publish aggregate evidence

1,500 curated rules in a checksum-verified public exhibit, with no raw customer identifiers.

What was excluded, and why?

30,317 routine line exclusions and another 5,773 eligible sale lines across conflicting invoices were excluded from the analytical cohort. Full definitions and precedence are documented in the methodology and reproducible code.

03 / THE DECISION

A sensible next experiment.
Not a promised return.

The repeat pattern is a reason to investigate the pairing, not to change a store layout without a controlled test.

A / Check feasibility

Stock and economics first.

Confirm that both products are available, compatible and viable after margin, return and placement costs.

B / Test a hypothesis

Compare offer and control.

Randomise eligible exposure to optional adjacent placement or bundling. Define sample size and allocation before launch.

C / Measure the effect

Look beyond association.

Measure completed purchases and contribution margin. Monitor inventory, returns and customer-experience guardrails.

Evidence boundary

This project demonstrates historical co-occurrence, not incremental revenue, causal conversion uplift, profitability or current consumer preferences.
